Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

UrbanFlow ("we", "us", or "our") is an agency operations and business management application operated by Urban Advertising. This Privacy Policy explains how UrbanFlow collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application, web platform, and associated services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your role and usage of UrbanFlow (as an Administrator, Agency Employee, or Client Representative), we may collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, work email address, phone number, and profile avatar.
  • Account & Organization Data: Account credentials, designated user role, and associated client organization details.
  • Operational Data: Assigned tasks, deliverables, projects, activity logs, support tickets, and monthly performance reports.
  • Workforce Data: Attendance and check-in records through authorized workforce management integrations.
  • Media & Documents: Photos, creative assets, audio recordings (for voice notes/tasks), and business documents uploaded through the app.
  • Marketing & Analytics Insights: Social media metrics, page insights, and ad campaign performance from authorized external integrations (e.g. Meta Graph / Marketing APIs).
  • Technical & Device Information: IP address, device model, operating system version, push notification tokens, and security access logs.
We collect only information that is strictly necessary to provide, optimize, and secure the application's agency management functionality.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following business purposes:

  • Authenticate users and enforce role-based access control.
  • Provide core agency operations, task delegation, and client servicing portals.
  • Track project workflows, deliverable approvals, and generate client performance reports.
  • Facilitate real-time communication, tickets, and audio updates.
  • Display authorized marketing analytics and ad budget metrics.
  • Send critical in-app and push notification alerts (e.g., task deadlines, payment receipts, plan expiry reminders).
  • Maintain system security, audit trails, and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose technical issues and continuously enhance application performance.

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third parties.

Information may only be shared with trusted third-party infrastructure providers necessary to operate UrbanFlow, including:

  • Secure cloud database and server hosting infrastructure (e.g., AWS, Azure).
  • Push notification and real-time messaging delivery services.
  • Authorized external integrations (e.g., Meta / Instagram Marketing API) strictly to retrieve campaign performance requested by the client.

We may also disclose information if required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or valid legal processes.

4. Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data, including:

  • End-to-end HTTPS/TLS encrypted communication between mobile apps and servers.
  • Industry-standard JWT authentication and encrypted password hashing.
  • Restricted, role-based access controls to prevent cross-account data exposure.
  • Protected server-side databases with regular automated backups.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill business purposes, deliver agency services, and satisfy legal or accounting obligations.
