Account & Data Deletion Request

Instructions for requesting deletion of your UrbanFlow user account and personal data.

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and data protection regulations, UrbanFlow provides users with full control over their account data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Via Email Request: Send an email from your registered work email to:
    privacy@urbanadvertising.in or urbanadvertising07@gmail.com
  2. Subject Line: UrbanFlow Account Deletion Request
  3. Include: Your Full Name, Registered Email Address, and your Role (Client / Employee).
  4. Processing Time: Our administrative team will verify your identity and process the deletion within 7 business days.
Send Deletion Email

What Data Will Be Deleted:

  • User authentication credentials and session tokens.
  • Profile details (Name, personal email, phone number, avatar).
  • Device tokens and push notification subscriptions.
  • Personal activity preferences and draft uploads.

What Data May Be Retained:

Certain operational records (such as completed financial invoices, contracts, and tax compliance data) may be retained for the period mandated by applicable corporate and accounting laws.
